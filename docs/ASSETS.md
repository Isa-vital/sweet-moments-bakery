# Visual asset notes

All four bakery images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool on 30 October 2026 for this coursework project. They are illustrative and must not be described as photographs of a real Sweet Moments Bakery, its staff, premises or products.

## Files and prompt summaries

- `hero-celebration`: premium editorial still life of an ivory-and-blush celebration cake and small pastries; wide landscape composition; negative space on the left; warm window light; no people, text, logo or watermark.
- `artisan-breads`: photorealistic golden sandwich loaf, rustic round loaf and rolls on a warm ivory bakery table; morning light; no people, text, logo or watermark.
- `cupcakes-muffins`: elegant blush and chocolate cupcakes and muffins on pale stone; boutique bakery lighting; no people, text, logo or watermark.
- `pastries-assortment`: glazed doughnuts, butter pastries and small cookies in a refined tasting spread; warm editorial light; no people, text, logo or watermark.

The original PNG files are retained as editable source assets. Optimised WebP derivatives are used by the website for better performance.
