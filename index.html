Our story

Sweetness with a sense of occasion.

Sweet Moments Bakery is a proposed Kampala-based bakery created around one simple idea: good bakes help people mark meaningful moments and brighten ordinary days.

The concept brings celebration cakes, familiar favourites and practical business snack options into one warm, easy-to-explore experience.
