How to order

Four steps, with confirmation built in.

The workflow is intentionally informational. A real business contact has not been configured.

  1. 1

    Browse the products

    Explore the proposed catalogue, descriptions and prices in Ugandan Shillings.

  2. 2

    Select a product

    Note the item and quantity you would like to discuss. No cart or checkout is active.

  3. 3

    Use the approved channel

    In a future live operation, a configured WhatsApp contact would open a pre-filled enquiry. No number is published in this demo.

  4. 4

    Confirm the details

    Availability, design, quantity, total price, delivery fee, timeline and payment arrangement would all be confirmed before proceeding.

Payment verification

In a future live operation, confirmation would rely on the bakery checking its own mobile money, bank or licensed payment-provider records. A customer screenshot alone would not be treated as proof of cleared payment.

Proposed refunds

Eligibility would depend on the order status and circumstances. Approved refunds would return through an agreed traceable method after review. Personalised items already in production may not qualify unless faulty or not as agreed.

Proposed delivery

Delivery is planned for Kampala and surrounding areas. Availability, fee and timeline would be confirmed using the location, product, quantity, date and route conditions. No delivery is booked by this site.

Business customers

Planning for an office, school or event?
