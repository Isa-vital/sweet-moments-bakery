01 · Privacy

Privacy Notice

A future operating Sweet Moments Bakery might need limited customer information such as a name, contact number, order details, delivery location, payment reference and communication history.

Why information might be needed

  • To respond to an enquiry and confirm an order.
  • To arrange production, collection or delivery.
  • To check payment against the business’s own transaction records.
  • To resolve a question, complaint or refund request.
  • To keep financial or order records where lawfully required.

Access and sharing

Access should be limited to authorised people who need the information to fulfil or administer the order. Information should only be shared with service providers such as an agreed delivery partner or payment provider when necessary, lawful and appropriately protected. It should not be sold.

Retention and security

Order and financial records should be kept only as long as needed for the stated purpose, dispute handling and applicable record-keeping obligations, then securely deleted or anonymised. The future business should use proportionate access controls, secure devices and careful handling procedures.

Questions and rights

A future customer should be able to ask what information is held, request correction, raise a concern or ask about deletion where applicable. A verified business contact would be published before launch.

This proposed approach is intended to be developed consistently with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and related obligations. Independent legal guidance would be needed before operation.

02 · Using the service

Terms and Conditions

The current website is a classroom demonstration. Product images are illustrative and prices are proposed coursework figures. Nothing on the site constitutes a confirmed order, payment request or guarantee of availability.

Future orders

  • An order would become confirmed only after the product, quantity, final price, timing, collection or delivery arrangement and payment method are agreed through an approved channel.
  • Custom details should be reviewed carefully by the customer before production begins.
  • Availability and timelines may vary according to the product, quantity and date requested.
  • Customers should disclose relevant dietary or allergen questions directly; this website makes no ingredient or allergen claims.

Acceptable use

Visitors should not misuse the website, attempt to interfere with its operation or present its coursework content as proof of a live commercial transaction.

03 · Getting it to you

Delivery Policy

Delivery is proposed for Kampala and surrounding areas, subject to confirmation. A future delivery offer would depend on the exact location, route, product, quantity, preferred time and available transport.

  • Delivery fees and estimated timing would be disclosed before an order is confirmed.
  • A customer would need to provide an accurate location and reachable contact number.
  • Delays caused by traffic, weather, access restrictions or other circumstances should be communicated promptly.
  • Someone should be available to receive and inspect the order at the agreed location and time.

The current website does not calculate delivery fees, track drivers or book deliveries.

04 · When plans change

Returns and Refunds

Because baked products can be perishable and some may be personalised, proposed refund handling would depend on the order status and specific circumstances.

  • Problems should be reported through the approved business contact as soon as reasonably possible, with the order reference and clear details.
  • Where a supplied product is materially faulty or not as agreed, the future business would review an appropriate replacement, correction, credit or refund.
  • Personalised products already in production may not qualify for cancellation or refund unless faulty or not as agreed.
  • Approved refunds would be returned through an agreed, traceable method after verification.

Submitting a screenshot would not by itself prove that a payment cleared; the bakery would verify its own financial records.
