Proposed coursework policies

Clear information, written in plain English.

These policies describe future practices for Sweet Moments Bakery. They are student-project drafts, not legal advice or proof of verified compliance.

Privacy NoticeTerms & ConditionsDelivery PolicyReturns & RefundsContact Information

01 · Privacy

Privacy Notice

A future operating Sweet Moments Bakery might collect a customer name, contact number, order details, delivery location, payment reference and communication history.

Why information might be needed

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm orders.
  • To arrange production, collection or delivery.
  • To check payment against business transaction records.
  • To resolve a question, complaint or refund request.

Access and retention

Access should be limited to authorised people who need the information to fulfil or administer the order. Records should be kept only as long as needed for the stated purpose, dispute handling and applicable record-keeping duties, then securely deleted or anonymised.

Questions

A future customer should be able to request access or correction and raise a privacy concern through the approved business contact. The approach should be reviewed against Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 before operation; this draft does not claim legal compliance.

02 · Website & orders

Terms and Conditions

This website is a classroom demonstration. Product images are illustrative and prices are proposed examples. Nothing on the site constitutes a confirmed order, payment request or guarantee of availability.

  • An order would be confirmed only after product, quantity, price, timing, delivery or collection and payment are agreed through an approved channel.
  • Customers should review all custom details before production begins.
  • Availability and timing may vary by product, quantity and requested date.
  • Customers should ask directly about ingredients and allergens; this catalogue makes no ingredient or allergen claims.

03 · Delivery

Delivery Policy

Delivery is proposed for Kampala and surrounding areas, subject to confirmation. Availability would depend on the exact location, route, product, quantity, requested time and available transport.

  • Delivery fees and estimated timing would be disclosed before an order is confirmed.
  • A customer would need to provide an accurate location and reachable contact number.
  • Traffic, weather or access restrictions may cause delays.
  • Someone should be available to receive and inspect the order at the agreed time.

04 · When plans change

Returns and Refunds

Because baked products can be perishable and personalised, proposed refund handling would depend on the order status and specific circumstances.

  • Problems should be reported promptly with the order reference and clear details.
  • A materially faulty or incorrect product would be reviewed for a replacement, correction, credit or refund.
  • Personalised products already in production may not qualify for cancellation unless faulty or not as agreed.
  • Approved refunds would use an agreed traceable method after verification.

05 · Contact

Contact Information

No approved public phone number, email address or physical shopfront has been supplied for this proposed business. To avoid publishing invented or personal details, this demonstration does not show a live contact.

Before any future launch: add the approved business phone or WhatsApp number, business email, privacy contact and verified collection or service address where applicable.
