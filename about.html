Our proposed story

Baked for the moments people remember.

Sweet Moments Bakery is a student-developed bakery concept shaped around celebration, convenience and the simple pleasure of sharing something good.

Kampala, Uganda

The idea

A bakery concept with room to grow.

Sweet Moments Bakery is proposed as a Kampala-based baked-products and delivery business serving both individual and organisational customers.

The concept brings together familiar everyday bakes, celebration cakes and convenient snack options in a polished, approachable online storefront. Product discovery happens on the website, while a future approved WhatsApp channel would support personal confirmation before an order proceeds.

Important context: this website is part of the BUC3106 E-Commerce Capstone Project at Makerere University Business School. It does not claim a trading history, physical shopfront or active service.

Our mission

To make meaningful moments feel a little sweeter.

The proposed mission is to offer appealing baked products and a clear, convenient discovery experience for customers planning celebrations, everyday treats and group occasions.

Our vision

A trusted, recognisable bakery brand for Kampala.

The proposed vision is to grow into a dependable local choice for individuals, families and organisations, supported by thoughtful service and a strong digital experience.

What guides the concept

Values baked into the plan.

Who we hope to serve

From family tables to team meetings.

The proposed range is designed for both individual customers and organisations with practical bakery needs.

Individuals & families

Students, professionals, families and celebration planners.

Business customers

Offices, schools, cafés, restaurants and event planners.

Planned service area

Kampala and surrounding areas.

Any future delivery would depend on the confirmed address, route, product, timing and delivery fee. The website does not currently calculate or promise delivery.
