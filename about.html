Our proposed story

Baked for the moments people remember.

Sweet Moments Bakery is a student-developed business concept shaped around celebration, convenience and the simple pleasure of sharing something good.

The idea

A bakery concept with room to grow.

Sweet Moments Bakery is proposed as a Kampala-based baked-products and delivery business serving both individual and organisational customers.

The concept brings together familiar everyday bakes, celebration cakes and convenient snack options in a polished, approachable online storefront. Product discovery happens online, while a future approved WhatsApp channel would support personal confirmation before an order proceeds.

Important context: this website is part of the BUC3106 E-Commerce Capstone Project at Makerere University Business School. It does not claim a trading history, physical shopfront or active service.

Our mission

To make meaningful moments feel a little sweeter.

The proposed mission is to offer appealing baked products and a clear, convenient discovery experience for customers planning celebrations, everyday treats and group occasions.

Our vision

A trusted, recognisable bakery brand for Kampala.

The proposed vision is to grow into a dependable local choice for individuals, families and organisations, supported by thoughtful service and a strong digital experience.

What guides the concept

Values baked into the plan.

01

Warmth

An experience that feels welcoming, human and suited to joyful occasions.

Products & customers

From family tables to team meetings.

The proposed range includes bread, cakes, cupcakes, doughnuts, cookies, muffins, pastries and office snack boxes.

  • Families, students and working professionals
  • Schools, offices and event planners
  • Cafés and restaurants
  • Everyday treats and special occasions

Ordering & delivery model

Online discovery, personal confirmation.

The proposed partial e-commerce model lets customers browse online, then confirm product availability, payment and delivery through an agreed ordering channel.

Delivery is planned for Kampala and surrounding areas. Final availability, route, fee and timing would be confirmed for each order.
